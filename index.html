MEP Design Services

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing design for commercial, industrial and mission-critical buildings — coordinated, code-compliant and engineered to perform.

  • HVAC design & load calculations
  • Chilled & condenser water plants
  • BIM models & construction documents

02

Data Center Thermal & Liquid Cooling

Thermal engineering for high-density AI/ML compute, from the chip to the chiller — air, liquid and hybrid cooling.

  • Data hall airflow CFD
  • CDU, cold plate & manifold integration
  • Air-to-liquid transition planning
  • Thermal digital twins & telemetry validation
03

Hydronic & Piping Analysis

System-level hydraulic modeling of chilled water, condenser water and process cooling networks.

  • Hydraulic network modeling
  • Flow balance, pressure drop & pump selection
  • Failure & transient scenario analysis
04

Industrial CFD & Multiphysics

High-fidelity simulation for power generation, emissions control and industrial facilities.

  • Cooling tower & exhaust recirculation
  • SCR / de-NOx mixing & species transport
  • Thermal fatigue & structural life analysis
05

PE Review & Sealing

Independent engineering review and Professional Engineer sealing of engineering designs and calculations.

  • Drawing & calculation review
  • Code compliance checks
  • Third-party peer review
