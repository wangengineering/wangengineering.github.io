Data Center Thermal & Liquid Cooling

Thermal engineering for high-density AI/ML compute, from the chip to the chiller.

  • Data hall airflow CFD

System-level hydraulic modeling of chilled water, condenser water and process cooling networks.

  • Flow balance, pressure drop & pump selection
  • Failure & transient scenarios

Industrial CFD & Multiphysics

High-fidelity simulation for power generation, emissions control and industrial facilities.

  • SCR / de-NOx mixing & species transport
  • Thermal fatigue & structural life analysis
